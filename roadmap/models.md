# Roadmap: Communication, queries, learning and other models

Part of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), which explains what an entry is and when one is added or removed.

- **Lifting** ([#890](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/890)): applications of deterministic lifting, such as partition-number separations, and randomized lifting. Low-discrepancy deterministic simulation now covers every positive discrepancy exponent and arbitrary sufficiently large finite alphabets. The conventional minimum-complexity bound and literal bit-string interface are proved. Remaining directions are randomized simulation, round-sensitive refinements, and improved explicit constants.
- **Randomized query complexity of game trees** ([#3060](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3060)): for the AND–OR (NAND) tree on `n = 4 ^ k` leaves, Snir's `R₀ = O(n ^ (log₂ α))`, `α = (1 + √33) / 4`, and the matching Saks–Wigderson lower bound via Yao's principle for expected cost.
  Also bounded-error bounds for the same trees, and Saks–Wigderson's results for general read-once formulas and MIN/MAX game trees over integer leaves.
- **Number-on-forehead multiparty protocols** ([#3197](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3197)): cylinder intersections and discrepancy, the Chung–Raz cube bound, Babai–Nisan–Szegedy's `Ω(n / 4 ^ k)` and a sharper `Ω(n / 2 ^ k)` (source unverified) for generalized inner product, Grolmusz's `O(k n / 2 ^ k + k)` protocol for any finite set of players ([#3453](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3453)), Raz's matrix multiplication and NOF disjointness bounds, and `ACC⁰` and branching-program applications.
  A two-party companion is the Aho–Ullman–Yannakakis bound `D(f) = O(N⁰(f) N¹(f))`.
- **Exactly-N in the number-on-forehead model** ([#3424](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3424)): the star lemma, that protocol leaves are cylinder intersections, and the Chandra–Furst–Lipton lower bound `D(Exactly-N) → ∞` for three players via the corners theorem; the converse from corner-free colorings, upper bounds from Behrend's construction, quantitative corners bounds, and the `k`-player version.
- **Information complexity** ([#3243](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3243)): internal and external information cost of deterministic, private-coin and public-coin protocols, with `IC ≤ IC^ext ≤ CC` and equality on product inputs; distributional-error information complexity; additivity and the Braverman–Rao direct sum `IC_{μⁿ}(fⁿ) = n · IC_μ(f)`, with private sampling from conditional marginals; information equals amortized communication.
  Also the Bar-Yossef–Jayram–Kumar–Sivakumar `Ω(n)` disjointness bound via the information cost of AND, with conditional information cost under a non-product `μ`.
- **Number-in-hand protocols**: multiparty number-in-hand protocols, direct-product results, and reductions to streaming, sketching and cell-probe lower bounds.
- **Sign-rank and unbounded error** ([#3262](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3262)): the Paturi–Simon characterization of unbounded-error communication by sign-rank, and Forster's spectral lower bound, with approximate isotropic position and his exact isotropic-position lemma, giving `signRank(IPₙ) ≥ 2^{n/2}` and `UPP(IPₙ) ≥ ⌈n / 2⌉`.
  Also the Alon–Frankl–Rödl counting bound, `signRank(EQ) = 3` on four or more inputs, and the Razborov–Sherstov exponential sign-rank bound for an `AC⁰` function, separating `Σ₂^cc` from `UPP^cc`.
- **Dynamic cell-probe lower bounds** ([#3207](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3207)): by the chronogram method, the Fredman–Saks bound `t_q = Ω(log n / log(t_u (w + 1)))` for dynamic prefix parity with deterministic worst-case update depth `t_u` and `S ≤ 2 ^ w` cells, then amortized updates and Las Vegas randomization through cell-probe Yao averaging.
  Also Pătraşcu–Demaine's trade-off `t_q lg(t_u / t_q) = Ω(lg n)` and reductions to dynamic connectivity.
- **Streaming and sketching**: native time and space bounds for Count-Min with hash-seed storage and evaluation; the AMS sketch for `F₀`, the AMS `F_k` estimator for streams of unknown length, and the `n^{Ω(1)}` lower bounds for `F_k` with `k ≥ 6`; `ProbComp` samplers and native realizations of the AMS sketches and CountSketch.
  Also turnstile updates as stream items, merging of Misra–Gries summaries, heavy-hitter recovery over the whole universe (dyadic or group-testing schemes) and heavy-hitter space lower bounds, and multi-pass communication-to-streaming transfers with pass counts.
- **Join-size sketches** ([#3551](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3551)): the Alon–Gibbons–Matias–Szegedy tug-of-war join signature over turnstile streams with insertions and deletions, and their signature-size lower bound for join sizes (Theorem 4.3).
- **SQL query semantics** ([#5282](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/5282)): an executable semantics for a SQL fragment with `NULL`, three-valued predicates, resolved column references and bag-semantics queries, adapting Evelyn Mitchell's `sql_equiv`.
  Ordered, bag and set equivalence of queries with proved transformation laws, a proof-producing simplifier, and equijoin row counts as the streaming join size.
- **Distinct elements** ([#3340](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3340)): the space-optimal `F₀` estimators (Bar-Yossef et al. Theorems 2–3, Kane–Nelson–Woodruff `O(ε⁻² + log n)`) and the `Ω(ε⁻²)` and `Ω(log n)` lower bounds for approximate `F₀`.
- **Locality-sensitive hashing** ([#3361](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3361)): MinHash, sensitive families, AND/OR/banding and S-curve gap, bit sampling, measure-theoretic LSH, SimHash, `p ∈ {1, 2}` `p`-stable LSH (`ℓ₁` Cauchy, `ℓ₂` Gaussian), and the `(c, r)`-ANN structure (`e⁻¹ + 1/3` failure), realized on the word RAM with hashed buckets, seed storage and native `n^ρ` query and preprocessing time.
  Also `p ∈ (0, 2) \ {1, 2}` `p`-stable LSH, smaller min-wise families, and LSH lower bounds (O'Donnell–Wu–Zhou, source unverified).
- **Dimension reduction** ([#2996](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2996)): the Johnson–Lindenstrauss lemma for dense `±1` sign matrices, extended to Achlioptas's sparse `{-√3, 0, √3}` distribution, Gaussian and spherical projections, and fast and sparse JL transforms; the Larsen–Nelson lower bound `k = Ω(ε⁻² log n)`; and JL maps as streaming sketches with `ProbComp` samplers and native costs for applying the projection.
- **Learning**: machine-certified running times for the LMN and Kushilevitz–Mansour learners; distributional problems and reductions, samplability, average-to-worst-case connections, PAC/agnostic learning, learning lower bounds, compression and refutation.
  Develop low-degree likelihood-ratio and sum-of-squares comparisons where justified, distinguishing restricted-model bounds from unrestricted hardness.
  Use the [distributional framework](complexity.md#average-case-complexity).
- **Realizable PAC learning** ([#2868](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2868)): the VC ε-net upper bound `O((d log (1/ε) + log (1/δ))/ε)` for general well-behaved classes over measurable domains, beyond countable domains.
- **Fundamental theorem of statistical learning** ([#4912](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4912)): for binary classes, the equivalence of uniform convergence, agnostic and realizable PAC learnability, learnability by ERM and finite VC dimension (Shalev-Shwartz–Ben-David, Theorem 6.7) beyond countable domains, with the remaining lower bounds and two-sided sample-complexity bounds of their Theorem 6.8.
  The converse direction uses the Ehrenfeucht–Haussler–Kearns–Valiant lower bound, vendored from its unsubmitted CSLib branch rather than reproved.
- **Agnostic learning of infinite classes on uncountable domains** ([#3005](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3005)): agnostic learnability of finite VC classes, at the sharp `O((d + log (1/δ))/ε²)` rate, is proved on countable domains with measurable singletons. Uncountable domains need a measurability hypothesis on the class.
- **Nonuniform learning** ([#4915](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4915)): structural risk minimization over a countable union of classes with uniform convergence, the minimum-description-length bound for a prefix-free description language via Kraft's inequality, nonuniform learnability of countable classes, the characterization of nonuniformly learnable classes as countable unions of finite-VC classes, and consistency.
- **Sample compression** ([#4914](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4914)): compression schemes and the Littlestone–Warmuth generalization bound, giving PAC learnability from a bounded-size scheme, and compression schemes from mistake-bounded online learners.
  Also the Moran–Yehudayoff converse from finite VC dimension, agnostic compression and unlabelled compression.
- **Boosting** ([#4913](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4913)): AdaBoost and its training-error bound `∏ₜ 2√(εₜ (1 - εₜ)) ≤ exp (-2 γ² T)` under the `γ`-weak-learning assumption, then generalization of the boosted class through the VC dimension of weighted majorities or the convex-hull margin bound, and weak-to-strong PAC learnability.
- **PAC-Bayes** ([#4916](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4916)): the Donsker–Varadhan change of measure and McAllester's bound for every posterior against a prior fixed before the sample, first over countable hypothesis spaces and then over measurable ones.
  Also the Maurer–Seeger `kl` form and Catoni's bound.
- **Rademacher complexity** ([#3297](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3297)): Massart's lemma, symmetrization, McDiarmid generalization bounds with expected and empirical Rademacher complexity, growth-function and VC bounds for `{-1, +1}` classes, the 0-1 loss relation, the resulting growth-function, VC and agnostic ERM bounds for binary classification, Talagrand's contraction lemma, and fixed-margin `ρ`-margin generalization bounds, on finite and then on general measurable domains.
  Also margin bounds uniform in `ρ` and local Rademacher complexity.
- **Linear predictors** ([#4919](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4919)): the `B R / √m` Rademacher bound for `ℓ₂`-bounded linear classes and the resulting SVM margin bound, and VC dimension `d` for homogeneous and `d + 1` for affine halfspaces in `ℝᵈ`.
  Also the `ℓ₁` bound `B R √(2 log (2 d) / m)` and kernel classes.
- **Stability and regularization** ([#4917](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4917)): true and empirical risk for general losses, the replace-one identity between on-average stability and the expected generalization gap, `2 ρ² / (λ m)` stability of Tikhonov-regularized loss minimization for convex `ρ`-Lipschitz losses, and the resulting learnability of convex-Lipschitz-bounded problems.
  Also smooth losses, high-probability stability bounds (Bousquet–Elisseeff, Feldman–Vondrák) and the stability of SGD.
- **Online-to-batch conversion** ([#4918](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4918)): averaging the iterates of an online convex optimizer run on i.i.d. examples, the resulting `B ρ / √T` excess-risk bound for projected SGD, PAC learners from mistake-bounded online learners by random stopping, and high-probability conversions by the Azuma–Hoeffding inequality.
  Also a high-probability SGD bound, `log T / T` rates for strongly convex losses and running-time bounds.
- **Multiclass and real-valued learning**: Natarajan and graph dimensions with the multiclass fundamental theorem, pseudo-dimension and fat-shattering dimension, covering numbers, and Dudley's entropy integral by chaining.
- **Cryptographic hardness of learning** ([#4927](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4927)): learners for a class containing a pseudorandom function family yield distinguishers, so no efficient learner, even with membership queries, learns polynomial-size circuits if secure generators exist; the converse of learning from natural proofs.
  Also the Kearns–Valiant, Kharitonov and Klivans–Sherstov hardness results, learning algorithms implying circuit lower bounds, and average-case hardness of learning versus one-way functions.
- **Statistical query learning** ([#2910](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2910)): the SQ model and its lower bounds, simulation from random examples, including under random classification noise of known and unknown rate (Kearns's Lemma 2 and Theorem 3), the single-sample form of Kearns's Theorem 1 and a distribution-free variant, and randomized SQ learners.
- **Statistical query dimensions** ([#2910](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2910)): the characterization of weak learning by SQ dimension ([#3204](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3204)), the Bshouty–Feldman transfer to correlational queries, the worst-case SQ dimension versus VC dimension (Reyzin's Observations 20 and 21), Feldman's strong SQ dimension, and his statistical dimension for a planted problem with matching sample and runtime upper bounds.
- **Online learning in the mistake-bound model** ([#2908](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2908)): the Littlestone dimension as the optimal mistake bound, WINNOW2 and attribute transformations (Littlestone 1988, Theorems 9 and 10), and optimal `Θ(√(Ldim(C) T))` agnostic regret ([#3120](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3120)) under adaptive adversaries and unknown horizons.
  Also multiclass and real-valued analogues and running-time bounds for the standard optimal algorithm.
- **Testing Boolean functions**: independent repetition of local tests and BLR linearity testing with explicit decision-tree query bounds, and further query lower bounds.
- **Monotonicity testing** ([#3257](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3257)): the Goldreich–Goldwasser–Lehman–Ron–Samorodnitsky edge tester for Boolean functions on `{0,1}ⁿ`, where squashing along each coordinate shows that a function `ε`-far from monotone violates at least an `ε / n` fraction of the edges, so `⌈2n / ε⌉` edge trials reject with probability at least `2/3` while monotone functions always pass.
  Also the `Ω(√n)` lower bound for non-adaptive one-sided testers (Fischer et al. 2002), larger ranges and hypergrids, and the directed-isoperimetry path testers with `Õ(√n / ε²)` queries (Khot–Minzer–Safra).
- **Distribution testing** ([#2930](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2930), [#3017](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3017), [#3122](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3122)): uniformity testing with matching `Θ(√n / ε²)` sample bounds on every domain for `ε ≤ 1/2`, identity testing by reduction to uniformity, and closeness testing with matching `Θ(n^{2/3} ε^{-4/3} + √n ε^{-2})` bounds.
  Also instance-optimal identity testing, tolerant and structured-property testing, further access models, and sample lower bounds.
- **Dense graph property testing** ([#3036](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3036), [#3180](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3180), [#3118](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3118)): `H`-freeness through removal lemmas, bipartiteness (Goldreich–Goldwasser–Ron, then sharper bounds) and `k`-colourability with `poly(1/ε)` queries, and Goldreich–Trevisan canonical testers with `O(q)` sample vertices and `O(q²)` queries.
  Testers should decide by the property itself when it is closed under induced subgraphs (Proposition D.2, due to Alon) or is a graph partition property (Corollary 5.9).
- **Kolmogorov complexity and metacomplexity** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798), [#4644](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/4644)): Liu–Pass Theorem 3.1 (c) in the near-linear regime, hardness for every polynomial `t(n) ≥ (1 + ε)n`, which needs a rate-1 efficient conditional EP-PRG running in `n + O(n^c)` multi-tape steps for a constant `c < 1`.
  Also prefix-free complexity, and resource-bounded MCSP variants with their connections to learning, cryptography and lower bounds.
- **Partial-MCSP hardness refinements** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): Hirahara's `(log N)^ε` inapproximability and distributional forms of partial-MCSP hardness, `NC¹-MCSP*`, `MKTP*`, and NP-hardness of learning programs (FOCS'22 Thm I.1).
  Also the `(log N)^ε` inapproximability of `MINKT*`, Liu–Pass's RAM `McK^tP`, ACMTV's `McKTP` and Hirahara's `MINcKT^SAT`.
- **Further metacomplexity developments** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): NP-hardness of the total problems `MINKT` and `MINcKT` is open and not planned, as is the reduction of `MINKT*` to `GapMINKT` (Hirahara, FOCS'22 p. 975).
  Also the reverse multi-tape-to-single-tape simulation and machine-backed Nisan–Wigderson decoding. For Multi-output MCSP, Ilango–Loff–Oliveira's MDCP hardness (their Section 3), search-to-decision reduction (their Section 4.3) and communication minimization problems (their Section 5) remain.
- **Learning from natural proofs** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): Carmosino–Impagliazzo–Kabanets–Kolokolova learn a class from a natural property useful against it.
  For `p = 2`, the learner from a dense test (Theorem 5.1 in a per-function black-box form) and its AC⁰[2] instance (Corollary 5.4, with queries and hypothesis size counted) are formalized.
  Theorem 5.1 in abstract form, odd `p` (GRS decoding over GF(p), the von Neumann step, Smolensky's dimension property), a machine-level runtime certificate (`IsOraclePPTOn`), compression (Corollaries 5.2 and 5.5) and the MCSP search-to-decision consequence remain.
- **Chaitin's incompleteness theorem** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): Chaitin's incompleteness theorem for the multi-tape plain complexity (which needs compiling partial recursive functions to programs of the universal Turing machine) and in a prefix-free version; Chaitin's halting probability `Ω` and its randomness, Solovay's refinements, and the Kritchman–Raz proof of Gödel's second incompleteness theorem.
- **Hardness magnification and the locality barrier** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): the locality barrier of Chen–Hirahara–Oliveira–Pich–Rajgopal–Santhanam (Theorem 59 and Corollary 61, ECCC numbering), which needs exponent-2 shrinkage under `k`-wise independent restrictions and Hirahara–Santhanam's `N^{2−o(1)}` formula lower bound for MCSP; the remaining bullets of their Proposition 63 (`[AK10]`, `[LW13]`, `[OS18]` `k`-VC and `[OPS19]` Gap-MKtP); and their Theorem 1 linking natural proofs, learning and pseudorandom functions.
- **Symmetry of information** ([#798](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/798)): the exact symmetry `K(x, y) = K(x) + K(y | x, K(x)) + O(1)` of prefix complexity (Gács, Chaitin), and symmetry of information for the binary-string models and for time-bounded complexity, with its connection to one-way functions (Liu–Pass, Hirahara).
- **Quantum computation** ([#896](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/896)): channel semantics and approximation costs alongside circuit and measurement semantics, reusing suitable quantum foundations rather than incompatible models; a query-to-circuit bridge; complete problems for BQP; related query, communication and interactive classes with amplification, containments and complete problems.
  Supply the [quantum adversary models](cryptography.md#concurrent-and-quantum-security) needed by cryptographic reductions.
- **Gate-set universality** ([#3350](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3350)): `BQP` over arbitrary elementary gates equals `BQP` over Hadamard, NOT, CNOT and Toffoli, through the exact real simulation of controlled phase gates, Kitaev's universality of `{Λ(P(i)), H}`, the Solovay–Kitaev theorem and polynomial-time synthesis.
  Also `BQP` with arbitrary elementary gates through Shi's constructive synthesis of real rotations.
- **QMA** ([#2876](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2876)): error reduction from an inverse-polynomial gap, strong error reduction that keeps the witness length, `QMA ⊆ A₀PP`, mixed witnesses, QCMA and the local Hamiltonian problem.
- **Quantum information primitives** ([#3329](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3329)): pure-state, ancilla-free no-cloning, single-qubit teleportation and superdense coding; the Wootters–Zurek form of no-cloning with an input-dependent apparatus register, and teleportation of one half of an entangled state, as in entanglement swapping (a pure-state statement).
  With channel semantics (CPTP maps), also no-cloning for mixed states and no-broadcasting.
- **Quantum information bounds** ([#3329](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3329)): over the density matrices, partial traces, no-signalling and quantum one-time pad of [#3490](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3490), the Ambainis–Mosca–Tapp–de Wolf bound that encrypting `n` qubits needs `2n` key bits, Holevo's bound, in particular the optimality of superdense coding, and Nayak's bound for quantum random access codes.
  All three use von Neumann entropy, and the last two also mutual information; the weaker key-count bound `N ≥ 4ⁿ` may admit a dimension argument.
- **Quantum query separations** ([#2991](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2991)): Simon's problem in decision and search versions, with polynomial-time post-processing by Gaussian elimination over `ZMod 2`, separations in the value-query model, and then Forrelation and other optimal quantum–classical query separations.
  The value-query model needs quantum states over an arbitrary finite query index type rather than `Fin N`.
- **Promise problems in quantum query complexity** ([#3310](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3310)): Deutsch–Jozsa's exact one-query algorithm against the deterministic `N/2 + 1` and constant-query randomized bounds; exact quantum lower bounds for promise problems via polynomial degree (`Q_E ≥ deg/2` on the promise domain); a promise form of `randomizedComplexity`; and recursive Fourier sampling (Bernstein–Vazirani).
- **Quantum query lower bounds** ([#3239](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3239)): non-Boolean input alphabets (Ambainis Theorem 7, permutation inversion, over the value-query model), comparison-tree, approximate-mean and relative-counting variants of Nayak–Wu, and oracle-overlap deduplication (Ambainis's relational adversary, Nayak–Wu consequences, and weighted/spectral/`ADV±` adversaries have landed).
- **Shor's algorithm** ([#3252](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3252)): bounded-error polynomial-time integer factoring and discrete logarithms in the native uniform quantum-circuit model, with explicit encodings, success probabilities and gate-approximation costs.
  It needs the quantum Fourier transform with its circuits, period finding including `r ∤ N` by continued fractions and the `Ω(1 / log log r)` totient bound, phase estimation, reversible modular exponentiation and the classical reductions, certifying uniform generation and polynomial gate bounds.
- **Nonlocal games** ([#3313](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3313)): CHSH and Tsirelson's bound in the finite-dimensional tensor-product model; for general XOR games, Tsirelson's vector characterization and its semidefinite program (Cleve–Høyer–Toner–Watrous Theorem 4, Proposition 5) and the Grothendieck-constant bound on quantum advantage (Theorem 7).
  Also mixed shared states and the commuting-operator model, with the inclusion of the tensor-product value; parallel repetition of entangled games, beside the classical theorem ([#886](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/886)); self-testing of the CHSH strategy; and the class `MIP*`, toward the optional [MIP* = RE](../ROADMAP.md#optional-landmark-projects) project.

### Differential privacy and adaptive data analysis

- **Differential privacy foundations** ([#2867](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2867)): dataset adjacency and `(ε, δ)`-differential privacy for probabilistic programs, with postprocessing and adaptive composition, including advanced composition ([#2977](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2977)).
  Mechanisms need both privacy and utility proofs with sampling cost and finite-precision error, covering exact sampling of discrete noise and continuous Laplace and Gaussian mechanisms.
  Also Rényi or concentrated privacy accounting.
- **Private query answering and learning** ([#2977](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2977)): private query answering and private learning, building on the exponential mechanism and the sparse vector technique; privacy–accuracy and sample-complexity lower bounds; then adaptive data-analysis and generalization guarantees.
  Keep the adjacency relation, interaction model, privacy parameters and computational resources explicit, reusing the probability, oracle-computation and learning APIs.
- **Privacy–accuracy lower bounds** ([#3178](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3178), [#3201](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3201)): the lower bounds of Dwork–Roth Chapter 8: the packing lemma, the `Ω(k / ε)` bound for one-way marginals, Dinur–Nissim reconstruction, reconstruction from `O(n)` random `±1` queries with `o(√n)` error (Theorem 8.2), and De's `Ω(min(k, d) / ε)` bound over a small universe (Theorem 8.8).
  This includes settling whether the source's constant `(2 α / η) ^ 2` in Theorem 8.2 holds for `η / 2 ≤ α < 1 / 2`, and Theorem 8.8 for add/remove adjacency on histograms as well as row replacement.
  Also `(ε, δ)` lower bounds via fingerprinting codes.
