# Roadmap: Pseudorandomness, coding and Boolean analysis

Part of the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), which explains what an entry is and when one is added or removed.

- **Improved sparse expanders** ([#3348](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3348)): simple degree-two and degree-three cores with their matching-path extensions, the link from their generalized eigenvector equations to normalized spectral expansion, explicit core families, and the density-sequence asymptotic coefficient approaching `π² / 8`.
  For lossless expansion, the equivariant regular subgraph and its full row rank through the orbit-matrix and prime-power-group argument.
  Finite spectral certificates, conditional cut-rank bounds and transfer for a supplied subgraph do not complete these construction endpoints.
- **Derandomization from hardness**: the infinitely-often variant, and the EXP ⊄ P/poly endpoint of Babai, Fortnow, Nisan and Wigderson.
- **Hard-core lemma** ([#3557](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3557)): the measure and set forms for nonuniform De Morgan circuits, with density `δ` from hardness `δ`; Holenstein's optimal density, a set of density `δ` from hardness `δ / 2` (his Lemma 2.1); the uniform hard-core lemma; and sharper size losses from smooth boosting.
- **Extractors** ([#895](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/895)): beyond Chor–Goldreich, two-source extractors for lower min-entropy through Chattopadhyay–Zuckerman's Theorem 4.1, resilient functions (Theorems 3 and 4) and extractor (Theorem 1); condensers, limited independence, explicit expanders and space-bounded generators.
  Finite guarantees stay separate from uniform construction and decoding costs.
- **Random graph connectivity** ([#3485](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3485)): for Mathlib's binomial random graph `G(n, p)`, connectivity with high probability when `n p - log n → ∞`, with the explicit bound `35 n exp (-(n p))` from a union bound over cuts, disconnection when `n p - log n → -∞` through the sharp threshold for isolated vertices by the first and second moments ([#3560](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3560)), and at the threshold `n p - log n → c` the Poisson limit for isolated vertices (via Bonferroni inequalities and Brun's sieve) and limiting connectivity probability `exp (-exp (-c))`.
  Then the hitting-time theorem and the translation to `G(n, m)`.
- **Random graph thresholds** ([#3275](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3275)): for Mathlib's binomial random graph `G(n, p)`, the first-moment `0`-statement for every fixed subgraph, the triangle threshold `p = 1 / n` by both moments with explicit bounds, the second-moment `1`-statement for balanced graphs at `n ^ (-v / e)`, and Bollobás's general threshold `n ^ (-1 / m(H))`.
  Also Janson's inequality and the Poisson limit at the threshold; the connectivity threshold `log n / n` belongs to random graph connectivity ([#3485](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3485)).
- **Explicit Ramsey graphs** ([#3041](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3041)): strongly explicit graphs with no `k`-clique or `k`-independent set on more than Nagy's `C(k - 1, 3)` vertices, toward Erdős's existential `2 ^ (k / 2)`: Frankl–Wilson's `k ^ Θ(log k / log log k)`, then the two-source disperser and extractor constructions of Barak–Rao–Shaltiel–Wigderson, Cohen, Chattopadhyay–Zuckerman and Li, with the disperser connection of [#895](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/895).
  Keep these bounds separate from polynomial-time recognition and adjacency certificates.
  An explicit graph on `2 ^ Ω(k)` vertices is open.
- **The Frankl–Wilson modular intersection theorem** ([#3041](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3041)): the theorem in its inclusion-matrix form (reportedly `|L| ≤ d`), beyond the restricted form `|L| ≤ d mod p` of the triangular polynomial argument.
- **Codes**: Guruswami–Sudan list decoding and local decoding, keeping interpolation, list-size/agreement guarantees and decoding runtime as separate obligations; concrete list-decodable codes for Nisan–Wigderson.
- **Upper bounds on code size** ([#3280](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3280)): the Singleton, Hamming, Plotkin (with its asymptotic form), alphabet-free and q-ary Johnson, and Elias–Bassalygo bounds, including the asymptotic Elias–Bassalygo bound `R ≤ 1 - H_q(J_q(δ)) + o(1)` through the volume lower bound `q ^ (H_q(e / n) n - o(n))` for q-ary Hamming balls.
  Also the McEliece–Rodemich–Rumsey–Welch linear-programming bound (Delsarte's linear program and Krawtchouk polynomials).
- **Expander codes** ([#3278](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3278)): Sipser–Spielman codes of bipartite vertex expanders with rate, distance and flip decoding; lossless expanders, probabilistic or explicit (Capalbo–Reingold–Vadhan–Wigderson), making expander codes asymptotically good; a machine-model linear-time decoder; Spielman's linear-time encodable codes; and Zémor's decoding of Tanner codes on spectral expanders.
- **Channel coding** ([#3304](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3304)): Shannon's theorem for the binary symmetric channel, with capacity `1 - h₂(p)` for every `p`: achievability by random codes with nearest-codeword decoding and maximal error `≤ 10 exp (-δ n)` below capacity, the weak converse via Fano's inequality, and the strong converse, with average error `≥ 1 - 2 exp (-δ n)` above capacity.
  Also dispersion terms at capacity, discrete memoryless channels with mutual-information capacity, feedback channels, explicit efficiently decodable capacity-achieving codes (Forney concatenation, polar codes), and the link to list decoding.
- **Locally decodable codes** ([#2929](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2929)): query-bounded decoders, the Hadamard code, and lower bounds through Katz–Trevisan smoothing for every adaptive decoder and the matching/XOR normal form: the one-query bound, the Katz–Trevisan bound `n ≥ Ω(k^{q/(q-1)})` with explicit `(δ, ε, q)` dependence, and the Goldreich–Karloff–Schulman–Trevisan and Kerenidis–de Wolf exponential bounds for two-query codes.
- **Lempel–Ziv compression** ([#3312](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3312), [#3518](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3518)): LZ78 incremental parsing with lossless pair and bit codes, the distinct-parsing bound and the worst-case length `⌈log₂ k⌉ n (1 + o(1))`; LZ77 sliding-window parsing with a lossless triple code and bit-level codes with bounded lookahead, the optimality and uniqueness of the exhaustive history, and `#LZ77 ≤ #LZ78`.
  Also asymptotic optimality for stationary ergodic sources through Ziv's inequality, and links to Kolmogorov complexity and finite-state compressibility.
  Machine-level or `TimeM` parser costs are a separate endpoint.
- **Boolean analysis** ([#2131](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/2131)): coverage of O'Donnell's *Analysis of Boolean Functions*, including the book-form statements of its Chapter 9 (the KKL edge-isoperimetric theorem, and Friedgut's junta theorem with its spectral concentration).
  Also hypercontractivity beyond `p ≤ 2 ≤ q` and its applications, such as small-set expansion and the Level-k inequalities.
- **Invariance principles** ([#3242](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3242)): from Chapter 11 of O'Donnell's book, the Basic Invariance Principle under Hypothesis 11.65 with Corollary 11.67, the Invariance Principle for `(2, 3, ρ)`-hypercontractive ensembles (Remark 11.66), the Lévy-distance form (Corollary 11.70), and cdf-closeness to Gaussians through the Carbery–Wright theorem (Theorem 11.71).
  Also Majority Is Stablest through Borell's isoperimetric theorem.
- **Resource-bounded measure and dimension** ([#3139](https://github.com/cslib-community/cslib-frontier/issues/3139)): Lutz's p-measure in `E` with the quantitative Measure Conservation bound and countable p-unions (Lutz's Lemma 3.10(c)), measure in `EXP` (`p₂`), p-randomness, gales and resource-bounded dimension.
  State the budgets and uniformity of betting strategies separately from measure-theoretic facts, and connect quantitative randomness to Kolmogorov complexity.
